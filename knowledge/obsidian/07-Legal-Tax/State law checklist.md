# State law checklist

A state law checklist is a review record for a specific jurisdiction, transaction structure, and effective date.

## 101XVC workflow

- Identify governing licensing, disclosure, brokerage, contract, and records rules
- Record counsel review and actual sources
- Track changes and effective dates
- Avoid applying one state's rule to another
