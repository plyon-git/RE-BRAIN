# Tax basis

Tax basis is an investment measure used for tax purposes and can change through adjustments. It is different from current market value.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record acquisition allocation and supporting settlement records
- Track capital improvements and basis adjustments
- Keep book and tax schedules separate
- Have the accountant resolve treatment before filing
