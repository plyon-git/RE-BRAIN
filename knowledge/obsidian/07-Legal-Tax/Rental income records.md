# Rental income records

Rental reporting requires accurate records of receipts, deposits, expenses, and property use under the applicable tax framework.

## 101XVC workflow

- Keep bank receipts and tenant ledgers reconcilable
- Separate refundable deposits from earned rent
- Identify personal-use or vacation-home facts
- Retain invoices and records for the tax professional
