# Disposition tax review

Tax consequences of a sale depend on basis, use, holding facts, character, recapture, and applicable rules.

## 101XVC workflow

- Compile sale proceeds and transaction expenses
- Reconcile basis schedules
- Separate tax estimates from pre-tax investment returns
- Have the tax professional review before reporting final after-tax outcomes
