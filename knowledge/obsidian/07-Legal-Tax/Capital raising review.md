# Capital raising review

Raising capital involves selecting a lawful offering pathway and maintaining accurate terms, records, filings, and disclosures.

## 101XVC workflow

- Document issuer, security, use of proceeds, and exemption
- Obtain review of financial claims and risk factors
- Track required filings and state obligations
- Keep signed subscription and cap-table records consistent
