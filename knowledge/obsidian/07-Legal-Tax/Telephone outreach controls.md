# Telephone outreach controls

Telephone outreach requirements depend on the actual campaign, calling method, recipients, and jurisdiction.

## 101XVC workflow

- Review federal and state requirements with counsel
- Keep consent and suppression records
- Use a documented calling-time policy
- Do not infer permission from a public phone listing
