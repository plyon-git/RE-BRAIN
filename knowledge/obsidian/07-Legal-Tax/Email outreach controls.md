# Email outreach controls

Commercial email operations need truthful presentation, appropriate sender information, and a reliable suppression process under applicable law.

## 101XVC workflow

- Review sender and subject accuracy
- Maintain opt-out processing and suppression records
- Keep list provenance and campaign versions
- Verify additional securities-marketing requirements for investor outreach
