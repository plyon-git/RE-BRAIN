# Investor reporting

Investor reporting distinguishes actual operating performance, cash distributions, accounting results, and forecasts.

## 101XVC workflow

- Reconcile reported numbers to the ledger
- Explain assumptions and material variances
- Disclose related-party fees and transaction boundaries
- Keep versions and delivery records
