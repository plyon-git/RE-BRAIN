# Exchange planning

A proposed exchange requires transaction-specific tax planning and timing controls. A profitable sale does not automatically qualify for deferral.

## 101XVC workflow

- Document asset use and transaction structure
- Obtain professional review before proceeds are received
- Track statutory and contractual deadlines from authoritative guidance
- Model liquidity and failed-exchange alternatives
