# Expense classification

An operating, capital, financing, or acquisition label should reflect the applicable accounting and tax policy rather than the desired NOI.

## 101XVC workflow

- Document repair and improvement facts
- Preserve invoices and scope
- Keep financing fees separate from property operations
- Reconcile management reporting with accountant classifications
