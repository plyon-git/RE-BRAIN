# Fair housing review

Housing-related decisions and communications should be reviewed against applicable fair housing obligations and documented business criteria.

## 101XVC workflow

- Use objective property and financial criteria
- Review screening and marketing features for improper protected-class use
- Keep decision explanations and correction channels
- Verify current federal, state, and local requirements
