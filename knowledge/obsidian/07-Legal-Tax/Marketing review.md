# Marketing review

Marketing claims should match the interest offered, actual authority, source evidence, and applicable advertising requirements.

## 101XVC workflow

- Verify ownership or contractual-interest wording
- Keep modeled returns labeled as projections
- Review property and investment claims separately
- Store the approved version and supporting evidence
