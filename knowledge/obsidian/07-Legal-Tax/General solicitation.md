# General solicitation

Offering rules affect how an issuer may contact prospective securities investors. The chosen exemption and actual conduct must be reviewed together.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record the selected pathway and counsel's requirements
- Review outbound campaign and public materials before launch
- Keep relationship and verification evidence
- Do not assume an investor directory grants solicitation permission
