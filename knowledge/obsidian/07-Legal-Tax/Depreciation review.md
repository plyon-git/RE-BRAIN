# Depreciation review

Depreciation depends on asset classification, placed-in-service facts, tax rules, and elections. Land and building allocations should be supported.

## 101XVC workflow

- Retain purchase and improvement allocations
- Record placed-in-service dates
- Separate repairs from capitalized improvements
- Use the current tax-year guidance with the accountant
