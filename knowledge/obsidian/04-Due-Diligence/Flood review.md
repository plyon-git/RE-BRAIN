# Flood review

Flood review starts with the actual parcel and applicable effective map. Absence from a mapped high-risk zone does not establish zero flood exposure.

## 101XVC workflow

- Save effective map and retrieval date
- Review amendments and elevation information
- Obtain an insurance quote using the actual property
- Inspect drainage and prior water incidents
