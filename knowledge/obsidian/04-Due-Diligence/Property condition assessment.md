# Property condition assessment

Condition review translates observed systems, deferred work, and useful lives into immediate repairs and future capital needs.

## 101XVC workflow

- Use a qualified inspector for the scope
- Separate urgent deficiencies from planned replacement
- Document sampling and access limits
- Match the report to scope, reserves, and contractor bids
