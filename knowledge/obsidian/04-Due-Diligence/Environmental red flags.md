# Environmental red flags

Former industrial use, tanks, unusual odors, stained soils, disposal records, and missing access are prompts for professional investigation rather than diagnoses.

## 101XVC workflow

- Describe observable facts without declaring contamination
- Check adjacent historical uses
- Escalate incomplete records to the consultant
- Hold uncertain cleanup costs as unresolved underwriting inputs
