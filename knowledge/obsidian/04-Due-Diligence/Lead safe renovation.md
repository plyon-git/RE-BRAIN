# Lead safe renovation

Renovation of covered older housing can create lead dust exposure and require certified work practices.

## 101XVC workflow

- Identify scope that disturbs painted surfaces
- Verify contractor and renovator credentials where required
- Budget containment and compliant cleanup
- Retain work and disclosure records
