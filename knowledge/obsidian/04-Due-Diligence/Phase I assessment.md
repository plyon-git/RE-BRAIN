# Phase I assessment

A Phase I environmental assessment is a structured professional investigation; it is not a universal guarantee that a property is uncontaminated.

## 101XVC workflow

- Document scope, timing, and intended reliance
- Review historical use and site reconnaissance limitations
- Require an environmental professional's conclusions
- Resolve material findings before relying on the report
