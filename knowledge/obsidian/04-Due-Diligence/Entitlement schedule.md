# Entitlement schedule

Entitlement timing includes review cycles, public processes, required studies, resubmissions, and conditions of approval.

## 101XVC workflow

- Identify each decision-maker and dependency
- Separate expected dates from guaranteed dates
- Track consultant deliverables and completeness
- Stress holding costs if approvals slip
