# Site access

Physical access and legal access are separate questions. Visible driveway use does not establish a recorded right to use it.

## 101XVC workflow

- Identify access to a public road
- Verify easement beneficiary and obligations
- Inspect practical emergency and construction access
- Check shared-access maintenance responsibilities
