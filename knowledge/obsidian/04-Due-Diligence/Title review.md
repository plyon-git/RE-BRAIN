# Title review

Title review examines the ownership interest and recorded matters affecting transfer or use. A county search does not replace a settlement professional's work.

## 101XVC workflow

- Compare vesting, legal description, and contract
- Read exceptions, liens, easements, and restrictions
- Assign responsibility for curative work
- Obtain updated settlement evidence before closing
