# Seller disclosures

Seller disclosures are evidence from a financially interested party. They should be reconciled with inspection, permits, and known records.

## 101XVC workflow

- Use the governing jurisdiction's required form
- Flag blanks and contradictory answers
- Preserve amendments and delivery date
- Require independent review for material condition claims
