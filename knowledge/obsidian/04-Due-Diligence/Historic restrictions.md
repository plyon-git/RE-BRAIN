# Historic restrictions

Historic designation can add review requirements affecting exterior work, materials, demolition, and project timing.

## 101XVC workflow

- Check parcel designation and district boundaries
- Determine the required preservation approvals
- Align contractor scope with approved work
- Include review delay and material costs in the budget
