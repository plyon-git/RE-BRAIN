# Lead disclosure

Lead hazard disclosures and renovation work requirements are distinct. EPA provides federal disclosure guidance for covered housing.

## 101XVC workflow

- Verify applicability from property age and transaction type
- Collect required known-hazard information and acknowledgments
- Keep delivered records with the signed agreement
- Check state and local overlays for the property
