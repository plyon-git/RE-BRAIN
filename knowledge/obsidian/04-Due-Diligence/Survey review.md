# Survey review

A survey helps establish boundaries, improvements, access, and possible encroachments for a specified property interest.

## 101XVC workflow

- Confirm survey date and covered parcels
- Match the legal description and title exceptions
- Review access and utility easements
- Resolve discrepancies with surveyor and closing counsel
