# Continuing environmental obligations

Environmental liability protections can depend on conduct after acquisition as well as pre-acquisition inquiries.

## 101XVC workflow

- Keep obligations in an ongoing asset checklist
- Track reasonable steps and institutional controls
- Record access cooperation and notices
- Have environmental counsel define parcel-specific requirements
