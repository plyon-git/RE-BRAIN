# Insurance review

Insurance terms affect both recurring expenses and loss exposure. Coverage cannot be inferred solely from premium price.

## 101XVC workflow

- Compare limits, exclusions, deductibles, and valuation basis
- Verify occupancy and renovation declarations
- Confirm lender requirements and bind date
- Stress an uninsured interruption and claim delay
