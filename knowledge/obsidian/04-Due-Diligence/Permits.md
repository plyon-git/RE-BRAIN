# Permits

Permits and inspections establish a documented approval process for specific work. A contractor estimate does not prove the work can legally proceed.

## 101XVC workflow

- Check existing and open permits
- List approvals required for the proposed scope
- Budget review, inspection, and correction time
- Retain final inspection and completion evidence
