# Natural hazard exposure

Regional risk indicators are screening inputs. They differ from property-level engineering, insurance underwriting, or a loss forecast.

## 101XVC workflow

- Keep geography and methodology version
- Avoid assigning county averages to an exact building
- Review relevant hazard-specific evidence
- Model deductibles, downtime, and uncovered repair costs
