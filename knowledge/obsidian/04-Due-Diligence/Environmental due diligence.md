# Environmental due diligence

Environmental due diligence evaluates contamination and related obligations. Qualified professional inquiry and continuing obligations are separate workstreams.

## 101XVC workflow

- Commission the appropriate assessment for the transaction
- Track recognized conditions and data gaps
- Budget follow-up testing and remediation
- Preserve the report, reliance rights, and review date
