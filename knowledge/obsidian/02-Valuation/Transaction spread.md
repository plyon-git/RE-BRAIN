# Transaction spread

Transaction spread is the difference between sale or assignment receipts and the specified acquisition and transaction costs. Its definition must be explicit.

## 101XVC workflow

- Define whether the figure is before or after acquisition expenses
- Distinguish gross contract spread from 101XVC net income
- Include failed-deal costs in portfolio economics
- Reconcile the realized result with the closing statement
