# Highest and best use review

A feasible alternative use must satisfy legal, physical, financial, and market constraints. An attractive rendering does not establish feasibility.

## 101XVC workflow

- Verify zoning and permitted use
- Assess utilities, access, and site constraints
- Model entitlement time and capital
- Compare viable alternatives with a common valuation date
