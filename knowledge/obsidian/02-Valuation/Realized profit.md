# Realized profit

Realized deal profit requires an explicit revenue and cost boundary. Positive sale proceeds alone do not prove profitability.

## 101XVC workflow

- Include acquisition, rehab, holding, financing, and selling costs
- State treatment of taxes and overhead
- Record cash timing for return calculations
- Keep projected, accounting, and realized cash measures separate
