# Scenario underwriting

A useful scenario changes coherent drivers such as price, rents, vacancy, repairs, interest, and duration rather than applying one arbitrary profit haircut.

## 101XVC workflow

- Build downside, base, and upside with explicit assumptions
- Show probability assumptions separately
- Stress combinations that can occur together
- Display cash shortfall and break-even thresholds
