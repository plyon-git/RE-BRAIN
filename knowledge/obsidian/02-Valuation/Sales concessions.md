# Sales concessions

Concessions can affect observed sale prices and effective transaction economics.

## 101XVC workflow

- Identify seller credits and financing incentives
- Separate ordinary local costs from unusual concessions
- Estimate market reaction with evidence
- Do not count the same concession in both price adjustment and cash flow
