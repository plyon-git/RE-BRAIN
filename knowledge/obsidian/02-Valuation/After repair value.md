# After repair value

ARV is an estimate of market value after a defined completed scope, not the highest nearby listing price.

## 101XVC workflow

- Specify finished condition, size, and legal use
- Use verified renovated comparables
- Resolve permit and addition assumptions
- Produce a range and sensitivity to market changes
