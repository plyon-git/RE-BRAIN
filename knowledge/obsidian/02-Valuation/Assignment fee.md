# Assignment fee

An assignment fee is transaction compensation under the actual assignment and settlement documents.

## 101XVC workflow

- Store payer, recipient, contractual allocation, and closing condition
- Do not equate an underwritten spread with earned fees
- Record fee adjustments and settlement deductions
- Reconcile earned, billed, and collected balances
