# Break even occupancy

Break-even occupancy is the occupancy needed for a specified cash-flow or debt-service objective under a stated rent and expense model.

## 101XVC workflow

- Separate fixed and occupancy-dependent expenses
- Include concessions and collection loss
- State whether reserves and debt service are covered
- Test rent declines as well as vacant units
