# Accounting gain

An accounting disposition gain compares recognized proceeds and accounting basis under the reporting entity's accounting policy. It is not a full investor cash return.

## 101XVC workflow

- Retain the source's measurement label
- Keep tax basis and original purchase price distinct
- Record entity-share versus asset-level amounts
- Do not derive cash IRR from a gain and sale price alone
