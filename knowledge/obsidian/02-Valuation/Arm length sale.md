# Arm length sale

An arm-length comparison is evaluated for normal market exposure and independently motivated participants rather than assumed from a recorded price.

## 101XVC workflow

- Review related parties and unusual concessions
- Identify distressed or portfolio transactions
- Check deed consideration against available closing evidence
- Flag transactions with unclear pricing allocation
