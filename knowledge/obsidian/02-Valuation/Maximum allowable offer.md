# Maximum allowable offer

A residual offer calculation starts with expected exit proceeds and subtracts repairs, financing, holding, selling costs, risk allowance, and required margin.

## 101XVC workflow

- Use itemized costs instead of an unexplained percentage
- Avoid double-counting financing and deposits
- Apply downside assumptions before setting a binding offer
- Compare the result with actual capital constraints
