# Net operating income

A consistent NOI definition separates property operations from financing, depreciation, corporate overhead, and gains on asset sales.

## 101XVC workflow

- Specify included income and expenses
- Reconcile to the operating statement
- Normalize one-time items with evidence
- Show reserves and capital expenditures separately
