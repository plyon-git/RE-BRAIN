# Cap rate

Cap rate equals a specified annual NOI divided by value. The income period, stabilization assumption, and expense definition must match the price.

## 101XVC workflow

- Identify trailing or forward NOI
- Compare assets with similar risk and rights
- Stress NOI and exit cap rate independently
- Avoid using a metro average as an exact property valuation
