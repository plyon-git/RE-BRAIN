# Valuation uncertainty

A valuation range should reflect data quality, comp dispersion, condition uncertainty, market movement, and model performance.

## 101XVC workflow

- Expose missing inspection and pricing evidence
- Compare alternative valuation methods
- Use observed error bands where validated
- Escalate assets outside the model's training domain
