# Equity multiple

Equity multiple equals total equity distributions divided by total equity contributions under a stated convention. It ignores how long the investment takes.

## 101XVC workflow

- Include follow-on capital calls
- Keep return of capital separate from profit distributions
- Exclude hypothetical residual value from realized multiples
- Pair the metric with time and IRR
