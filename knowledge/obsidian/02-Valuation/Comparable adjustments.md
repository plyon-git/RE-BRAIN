# Comparable adjustments

Adjustments estimate market reaction to differences. A construction cost or concession amount need not equal the value adjustment.

## 101XVC workflow

- Use supported paired comparisons when feasible
- Avoid mechanical price-per-square-foot corrections
- Document time and property-right differences
- Show unadjusted and adjusted values together
