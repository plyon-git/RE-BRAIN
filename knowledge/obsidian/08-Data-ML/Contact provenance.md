# Contact provenance

A contact record should indicate whether a phone or email was publicly listed, provided directly, verified, inferred, or outdated.

## 101XVC workflow

- Save the public source and observation date
- Do not present inferred addresses as verified
- Track suppression and update history
- Use business context and applicable outreach rules
