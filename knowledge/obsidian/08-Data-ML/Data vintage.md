# Data vintage

A vintage identifies the dataset release and revision state available at a particular time.

## 101XVC workflow

- Keep release date and observation period
- Avoid silently mixing revised and unrevised series
- Preserve the vintage used for historical decisions
- Recompute outputs deliberately after updates
