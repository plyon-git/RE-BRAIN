# Closed deal evidence

A closed-deal record needs explicit completion evidence and a precise profit measure. An asking price, signed contract, or sales volume is insufficient.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record actual asset and seller interest
- Keep reported closing period if the exact date is unavailable
- Separate gross price, net proceeds, basis, gain, and cash return
- Require complete dated cash flows before computing realized IRR
