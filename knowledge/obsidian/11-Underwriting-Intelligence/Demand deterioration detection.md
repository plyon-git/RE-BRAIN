# Demand deterioration detection

Changes in executable bids, partner approvals, concessions, renegotiations, and closing delays may reveal demand deterioration before completed-sale averages adjust.

## 101XVC workflow

- Monitor matched cohorts and comparable products
- Separate source coverage changes from market change
- Require adequate recent samples and uncertainty
- Record the evidence that triggers repricing or inventory review
