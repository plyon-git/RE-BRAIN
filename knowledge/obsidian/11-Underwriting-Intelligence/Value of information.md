# Value of information

Additional information has value when it can change a decision enough to justify acquisition cost, time, and delay exposure.

## 101XVC workflow

- List decisions that the missing evidence could change
- Evaluate plausible results and resulting actions
- Subtract inspection or document cost and holding delay
- Distinguish a heuristic priority score from a validated expected information value
