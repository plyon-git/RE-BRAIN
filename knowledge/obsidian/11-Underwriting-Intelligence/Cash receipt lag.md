# Cash receipt lag

A funded closing and the company receiving cash can occur at different times. Estimate the post-closing collection lag separately from time to closing.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record funded date, received date, and receipt amount
- Keep unpaid closed episodes censored for collection analysis
- Separate partial and final collections
- Show how funding delays affect the cash calendar
