# Time origin and landmark forecasts

A closing forecast needs a defined starting event. Updated stage-specific forecasts condition on surviving and reaching that stage with information available then.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record offer, contract, approval, and funded dates separately
- Avoid mixing elapsed contract time with remaining time
- Rebuild stage-specific risk sets without future evidence
- Display sample support at each forecast horizon
