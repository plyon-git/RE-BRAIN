# Capacity aware acquisition

A profitable-looking contract can be impractical when buyer funding, partner throughput, title work, or staff capacity is already committed elsewhere.

## 101XVC workflow

- Define observable capacity and commitment units
- Share demand constraints across competing episodes
- Reserve room for uncertainty and delayed exits
- Do not assume public buyer activity proves available capacity today
