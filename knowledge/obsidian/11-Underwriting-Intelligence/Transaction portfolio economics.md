# Transaction portfolio economics

Gross spread, partner share, retained fee, attributable costs, collections, funding needs, and loss exposure require separate fields and explicit timing.

## 101XVC workflow

- Reconcile actual receipts with settlement and bank records
- Model slower cash receipt and failed transactions
- Aggregate market and partner concentration
- Apply marginal allocation only after its incremental contribution assumptions are supported
