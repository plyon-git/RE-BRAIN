# Right censoring

An unfinished episode reveals that no terminal outcome was observed through its follow-up time. It is not evidence of a failed transaction.

## 101XVC workflow

- Choose an explicit observation cutoff
- Use elapsed time for open episodes
- Keep administrative censoring separate from loss to follow-up
- Examine whether follow-up loss relates to unobserved outcome risk
