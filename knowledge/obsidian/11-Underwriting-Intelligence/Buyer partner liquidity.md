# Buyer partner liquidity

An executable bid has price, terms, funds, timing, conditions, and expiry. Stated interest is a weaker evidence category.

## 101XVC workflow

- Track bids and capacity with observation dates
- Record partner approvals, rejections, pricing feedback, and settlements
- Expose limited buyer visibility
- Account for multiple properties competing for the same capacity
