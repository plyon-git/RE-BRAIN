# Settled economics reconciliation

A model target for realized contribution must reconcile retained receipts and attributable expenses across successful and failed episodes.

## 101XVC workflow

- Keep gross spread and partner allocation distinct
- Use actual collected amounts and their dates
- Include lead spending, transaction costs, and failed-contract loss exposure under a stated policy
- Leave missing material costs unresolved instead of substituting zero
