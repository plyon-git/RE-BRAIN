# Marginal marketing allocation

The return on the next acquisition dollar may differ from a market historical average because channels saturate, costs rise, and operational capacity changes.

## 101XVC workflow

- Keep dated spend and attributable outcomes by mature cohort
- Include cancellations and labor costs
- Estimate incremental response rather than extrapolating an average
- Use capacity and downside constraints before increasing budgets
