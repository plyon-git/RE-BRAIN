# Versioned transaction rules

Rules determine permitted actions and contractual requirements; models estimate outcomes. Statistical output cannot override an applicable rule or unreviewed exception.

## 101XVC workflow

- Select rules by jurisdiction, counterparty, contract, and effective date
- Store review status and source
- Preserve approvals and exceptions
- Keep assumption defaults distinct from reviewed legal or partner requirements
