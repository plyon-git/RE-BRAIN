# Learning decision ledger

A learning ledger preserves evidence available, prediction, recommendation, actual action, outcome, and settled economics as linked records.

## 101XVC workflow

- Make original decision snapshots immutable
- Record model, rules, features, and assumption versions
- Append corrections and outcomes instead of rewriting history
- Keep refusals, losses, and open episodes alongside wins
