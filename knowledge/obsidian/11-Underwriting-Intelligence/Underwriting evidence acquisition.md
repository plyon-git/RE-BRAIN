# Underwriting evidence acquisition

Public records enrich property context; internal offers, bids, milestones, expenses, and settlements establish how the acquisition operation actually performs.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record observed and available-at timestamps separately
- Attach raw evidence or source spans to material fields
- Track collector failures and stale records
- Require settlement evidence before labeling realized economics
