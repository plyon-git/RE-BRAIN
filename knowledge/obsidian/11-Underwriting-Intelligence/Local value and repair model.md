# Local value and repair model

As-is value, ARV, executable investor price, and repair cost are distinct prediction targets. A new county needs explicit uncertainty until local errors are measured.

## 101XVC workflow

- Train value estimates on verified comparable transactions
- Train repairs on itemized scopes and actual costs
- Keep regional pooling and local adjustments explicit
- Evaluate later observations by geography and condition
