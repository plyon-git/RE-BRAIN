# Offer negotiation optimization

Offer scenarios combine acquisition price, terms, expected receipts, execution probability, loss exposure, cash timing, and capacity constraints.

## 101XVC workflow

- Separate supplied scenario assumptions from validated estimates
- Rank opening, target, counteroffer, and ceiling alternatives by the stated objective
- Show losses and cash shortfalls
- Abstain from probability-weighted recommendations when essential estimates lack evidence
