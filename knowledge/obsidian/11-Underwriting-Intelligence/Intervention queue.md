# Intervention queue

A work queue can prioritize unresolved evidence and deadline exposure. Ranking a task does not establish the causal financial benefit of performing it.

## 101XVC workflow

- Identify document, access, inspection, bid, and partner-review blockers
- State why the forecast depends on each blocker
- Separate heuristic urgency from estimated treatment effect
- Record completion and subsequent outcomes without claiming causation
