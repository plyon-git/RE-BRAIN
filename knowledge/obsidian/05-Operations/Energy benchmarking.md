# Energy benchmarking

Energy benchmarking compares measured consumption under defined building-use and weather assumptions.

## 101XVC workflow

- Reconcile meters and whole-building coverage
- Store floor area, occupancy, and usage definitions
- Evaluate savings against installed cost
- Treat self-reported peer data as imperfect comparisons
