# Closing statement reconciliation

Closing reconciliation turns projected economics into a documented transaction result and distinguishes credits from cash movements.

## 101XVC workflow

- Match buyer and seller names to the agreement
- Reconcile sale proceeds, deposits, prorations, fees, and debt payoff
- Compare actual cash with bank records
- Explain every material deviation from underwriting
