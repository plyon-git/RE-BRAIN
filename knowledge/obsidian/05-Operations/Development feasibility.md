# Development feasibility

Development feasibility combines entitlement, site conditions, construction, financing, absorption, and exit economics.

## 101XVC workflow

- Map legal and physical constraints
- Price the complete development budget
- Model delayed stabilization and refinance
- Require updated evidence at each capital commitment
