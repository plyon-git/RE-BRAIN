# Rent roll

A rent roll is a dated unit or lease inventory. It must be reconciled to signed leases and collected income.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record unit, area, lease term, rent, concessions, and occupancy
- Distinguish billed from collected amounts
- Reconcile totals with accounting records
- Track delinquency and lease rollover separately
