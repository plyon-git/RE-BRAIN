# Construction schedule

A schedule should model dependencies, approvals, lead times, inspections, and critical work rather than a single estimated finish date.

## 101XVC workflow

- Track baseline and current forecast
- Flag critical-path delays
- Link schedule movement to carry costs
- Retain evidence of milestone completion
