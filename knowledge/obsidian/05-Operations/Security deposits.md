# Security deposits

Tenant deposits create reconciliation and transfer obligations under leases and local law. They are not automatically operating revenue.

## 101XVC workflow

- Reconcile deposit ledger to cash and liabilities
- Confirm transfer at acquisition
- Keep refunds and deductions documented
- Review jurisdiction-specific handling requirements
