# Operating efficiency

Operating efficiency improvements should be measured against a stable baseline while checking service quality and deferred-maintenance effects.

## 101XVC workflow

- Define the expense and output being improved
- Normalize for occupancy and weather where relevant
- Track implementation and maintenance costs
- Avoid counting cost shifting as true savings
