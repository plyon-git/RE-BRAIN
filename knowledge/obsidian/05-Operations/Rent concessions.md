# Rent concessions

Concessions reduce effective rent and may occur at move-in, renewal, or throughout a lease term.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record amount, timing, and lease duration
- Calculate effective rent consistently
- Compare new and renewal leasing separately
- Do not omit concessions from a lease-up cash forecast
