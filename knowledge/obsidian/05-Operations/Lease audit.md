# Lease audit

Lease audit verifies the legal and economic terms behind a rent roll, including concessions, renewal rights, deposits, and obligations.

## 101XVC workflow

- Sample or review leases according to materiality
- Compare terms with tenant ledger
- Track amendments and missing signatures
- Resolve discrepancies before relying on in-place rent
