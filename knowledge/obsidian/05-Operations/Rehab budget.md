# Rehab budget

A complete rehab budget includes hard costs, professional fees, approvals, contingency, financing, and carry through completion.

## 101XVC workflow

- Compare bids on identical quantities and scope
- Separate allowances from fixed prices
- Track committed, paid, and forecast-to-complete cost
- Update the exit model after every material change
