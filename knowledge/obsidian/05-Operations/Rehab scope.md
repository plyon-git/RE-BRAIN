# Rehab scope

A rehab scope specifies the work, quantities, standard, exclusions, permits, and acceptance criteria before a reliable budget can be set.

## 101XVC workflow

- Use room and system-level line items
- Tie finish quality to the target comps
- Identify concealed-condition assumptions
- Have the contractor acknowledge exclusions and dependencies
