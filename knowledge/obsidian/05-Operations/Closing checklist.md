# Closing checklist

Closing readiness means contractual, title, financing, physical, authority, and settlement requirements are resolved or explicitly accepted.

## 101XVC workflow

- Review the complete settlement packet
- Verify final funds and instructions independently
- Complete the agreed walkthrough
- Store signed documents and recorded conveyance evidence
