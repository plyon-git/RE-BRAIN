# T12 analysis

Trailing twelve-month analysis shows recent operating performance but may contain one-time items, vacancy changes, and incomplete accounting.

## 101XVC workflow

- Reconcile monthly statements to the annual total
- Tag recurring and exceptional items
- Verify taxes, insurance, payroll, and utilities
- Show historical and normalized NOI separately
