# Replacement reserves

Replacement reserves fund future capital items and should reflect condition, useful life, financing terms, and the business plan.

## 101XVC workflow

- Use a system-level replacement schedule
- Separate deposit contributions from actual capital spending
- Track reserve restrictions and withdrawals
- Avoid treating required lender deposits as free operating cash
