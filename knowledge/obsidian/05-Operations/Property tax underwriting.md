# Property tax underwriting

Property taxes can change after acquisition, reassessment, rate changes, exemptions, or expiring incentives.

## 101XVC workflow

- Verify assessed and taxable values independently
- Use the actual local taxing entities
- Model a reassessment scenario
- Do not assume the seller's tax bill continues indefinitely
