# Economic occupancy

Economic occupancy compares actual or earned rental income with a defined gross potential rent measure. Different conventions can produce different ratios.

## 101XVC workflow

- State whether income is billed or collected
- Separate concessions and bad debt
- Reconcile potential rent to unit inventory
- Avoid comparing physical and economic ratios as interchangeable
