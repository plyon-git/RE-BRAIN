# Contractor selection

Contractor selection weighs relevant capacity, credentials, insurance, scope understanding, references, and payment terms.

## 101XVC workflow

- Verify licensing and credentials through official channels
- Ask for similar completed projects
- Review subcontracting and lien-release processes
- Require a written scope and acceptance criteria
