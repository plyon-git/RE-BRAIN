# Rehab contingency

Contingency is reserved capacity for defined uncertainty. It should not mask known work omitted from the estimate.

## 101XVC workflow

- List risks that the contingency covers
- Separate owner changes from hidden conditions
- Keep approval and remaining reserve visible
- Stress overruns beyond the allowance
