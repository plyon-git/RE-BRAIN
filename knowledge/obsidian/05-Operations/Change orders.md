# Change orders

A change order records a scope, cost, or schedule change and who authorized it.

## 101XVC workflow

- Require written pricing and reason before work where feasible
- Link the change to the original line item
- Update completion forecast and contingency
- Keep cumulative changes visible to the investment reviewer
