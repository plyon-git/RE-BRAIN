# Lease up plan

A lease-up forecast links delivery, marketing, applications, signed leases, move-ins, effective rent, and collection timing.

## 101XVC workflow

- Model unit availability by month
- Use supported rent and absorption assumptions
- Include concessions and marketing spend
- Stress a slower pace while fixed costs continue
