# Physical occupancy

Physical occupancy counts occupied units or space under an explicit definition. It does not show whether full rent is being collected.

## 101XVC workflow

- Fix the observation date and denominator
- Separate offline units from leased vacant units
- Track lease-up and move-outs
- Compare with economic occupancy and collections
