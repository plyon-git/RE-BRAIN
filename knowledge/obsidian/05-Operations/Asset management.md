# Asset management

Asset management checks whether actual operations, capital work, financing, and disposition remain consistent with the approved business plan.

## 101XVC workflow

- Review budget versus actual monthly
- Track unresolved physical and legal obligations
- Update lease rollover and debt calendar
- Document decisions and resulting cash impact
