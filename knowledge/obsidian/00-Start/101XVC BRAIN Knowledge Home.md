## What is included

182 original operational knowledge notes, 10 operating templates, 53 completed-transaction case records, and 70 linked primary references.

Cases distinguish gross sales price, asset valuation, seller share, accounting gain, period aggregates, and non-cash contributions. The disclosed positive-gain selection is not a proof of complete net cash profitability. Original acquisition cost and complete cash flows remain unknown unless independently documented.

## Use

Open this folder as a vault in Obsidian. No community plugins are required. Wikilinks, Markdown, YAML metadata, JSON manifests, and CSV deal exports work without hosted AI. The core notes can also be read with any text editor and indexed by local NLP.

Start with [[Property intake template]] and [[Investment committee template]]. When researching a completed deal, use [[Deal evidence template]] and [[Closed deal evidence]].

## Evidence policy

Original prose and selected factual transaction fields are included. Entire publisher pages, paywalled material, user financial agreements, and guessed outcomes are not redistributed. References are dated and should be refreshed for decisions involving current law, financing policy, or market conditions. A source link is evidence about the referenced domain, not a blanket endorsement of every operating procedure.
