# Levered cash flow

Levered cash flow includes financing receipts, debt service, refinancing, and final debt repayment, with the resulting equity cash flows.

## 101XVC workflow

- Start with unlevered property cash flow
- Keep borrowed proceeds out of operating income
- Include fees, reserve accounts, and repayment
- Reconcile equity cash movements to bank records
