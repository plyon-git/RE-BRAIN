# Refinance risk

A maturing loan can fail to refinance even when operations continue, because value, income, rates, liquidity, and lender appetite change.

## 101XVC workflow

- Recalculate debt sizing at maturity
- Model required equity paydown
- Document extension conditions and contingency liquidity
- Track tenant rollover before the refinancing window
