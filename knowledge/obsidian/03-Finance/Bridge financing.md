# Bridge financing

Bridge financing covers a transition such as repair, lease-up, or a future sale. The exit and interim liquidity must be modeled explicitly.

## 101XVC workflow

- Document the business-plan milestone financed
- Include origination, exit, and extension fees
- Stress a delayed exit
- Identify cash needed if permanent financing is smaller than expected
