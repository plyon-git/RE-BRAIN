# Balloon maturity

A balloon is principal still due when a loan matures. Its repayment depends on cash, a sale, or a new financing transaction.

## 101XVC workflow

- Compute maturity balance from the actual schedule
- Set refinance milestones before maturity
- Stress lower value and tighter loan sizing
- Keep extension rights and fees documented
