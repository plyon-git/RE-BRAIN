# Loan to cost

LTC compares debt with a documented project cost basis. Eligible cost definitions and disbursement mechanics come from loan terms.

## 101XVC workflow

- Break out land, hard costs, soft costs, fees, and reserves
- Confirm equity-first or pro-rata funding requirements
- Track budget overruns outside the borrowing base
- Compare funded balance with committed exposure
