# Guarantee review

Guarantees can create payment, completion, environmental, or misconduct-related obligations. The actual signed scope is the relevant evidence.

## 101XVC workflow

- Identify guarantor and covered obligations
- Record limits, triggers, survival, and release conditions
- Avoid assuming nonrecourse means no contingent obligations
- Have counsel reconcile guarantees with the cash model
