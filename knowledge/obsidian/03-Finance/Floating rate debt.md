# Floating rate debt

Floating-rate debt changes payment or accrued interest with a specified reference rate, spread, reset schedule, and contractual floors.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record the exact rate formula
- Model the reset calendar and floor
- Include hedge costs and expiration
- Stress cash needs before assuming a future refinance
