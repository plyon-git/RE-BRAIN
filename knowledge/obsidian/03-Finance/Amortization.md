# Amortization

Amortization is scheduled principal reduction. Loan term and amortization period may differ, creating a balloon balance.

## 101XVC workflow

- Store payment frequency and day-count convention
- Separate principal from interest in cash flows
- Calculate the balance at maturity
- Test the first payment after any interest-only period
