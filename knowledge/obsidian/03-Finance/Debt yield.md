# Debt yield

Debt yield is a stated annual NOI divided by debt principal. It measures income relative to loan exposure without depending directly on the interest rate.

## 101XVC workflow

- State the NOI definition and loan balance date
- Use the same income period across alternatives
- Compare with DSCR and LTV rather than replacing them
- Stress capital repairs separately
