# Working capital

Working capital is the cash required to execute before collections arrive. Forecast gross cash uses and receipts rather than relying on a profit total.

## 101XVC workflow

- Use a monthly or weekly cash calendar
- Include deposits, payroll, lead costs, repairs, and failed deals
- Track committed and available capital separately
- Stress closing delays and receivable collection
