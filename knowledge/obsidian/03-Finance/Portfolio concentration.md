# Portfolio concentration

Similar geography, tenants, property types, lenders, and counterparties can create correlated downside even across many properties.

## 101XVC workflow

- Aggregate exposure across related entities
- Show concentration by capital at risk and expected receipts
- Stress a common shock across assets
- Set documented limits and exception authority
