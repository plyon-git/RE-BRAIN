# Loan sizing

Loan proceeds can be constrained simultaneously by value, cost, debt yield, DSCR, liquidity, and lender policy.

## 101XVC workflow

- Calculate each constraint independently
- Use the lowest applicable supported result
- Include fees and required reserves in usable proceeds
- Re-run sizing under refinance and downside assumptions
