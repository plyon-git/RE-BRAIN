# Capital stack

The capital stack organizes claims such as senior debt, subordinate debt, preferred equity, and common equity by rights and economic priority.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record lien and distribution priorities
- Map control and default rights
- Model total leverage rather than only senior LTV
- Identify personal and entity guarantees
