# Debt service coverage

DSCR equals the lender-defined income or cash-flow measure divided by debt service. Compare ratios only when both definitions match.

## 101XVC workflow

- Use principal and interest required during the period
- Identify reserve deductions in the numerator
- Stress floating rates and income declines
- Treat covenant minimums as contract-specific inputs
