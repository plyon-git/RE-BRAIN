# Interest rate caps

An interest-rate cap has a notional, strike, reference rate, expiry, premium, and counterparty. It does not eliminate every financing risk.

## 101XVC workflow

- Match hedge terms to the loan
- Account for premium funding
- Track replacement requirements and expiration
- Model uncovered exposure beyond the cap term
