# Building permits data

Building permits measure authorizations for covered construction. They are different from starts, completions, deliveries, and available inventory.

## 101XVC workflow

- Keep units separate from building counts
- Record survey geography and reporting vintage
- Compare authorizations with starts and completions
- Do not assume every permitted unit will be delivered on time
