# Vacancy statistics

Vacancy estimates depend on survey population, geography, period, and definitions. Different Census surveys should not be mixed casually.

## 101XVC workflow

- State the survey and vacancy definition
- Keep rental and homeowner vacancy distinct
- Review uncertainty and comparability
- Use property-level rent rolls for the subject asset
