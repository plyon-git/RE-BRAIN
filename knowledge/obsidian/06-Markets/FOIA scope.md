# FOIA scope

Federal FOIA is a process for federal agency records. County tax rolls typically require the relevant state or local process.

## 101XVC workflow

- Identify the actual records custodian
- Do not demand creation of a new analysis under a records request
- Keep requested records and requested explanations separate
- Record the governing law and response status
