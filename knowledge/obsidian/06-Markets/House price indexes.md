# House price indexes

A house-price index measures change for a specified dataset and methodology. It is not an appraisal of every home in that geography.

## 101XVC workflow

- Retain index type and base period
- Separate seasonally adjusted and unadjusted series
- Keep release and observation dates
- Use the index as market context rather than an exact ARV
