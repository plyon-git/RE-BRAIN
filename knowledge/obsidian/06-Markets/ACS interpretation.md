# ACS interpretation

ACS estimates carry sampling uncertainty and cover a defined period. A five-year estimate is not a snapshot of the final year alone.

## 101XVC workflow

- Save dataset vintage and variable definitions
- Retrieve estimates with margins of error
- Compare like periods and geographies
- Do not overinterpret small differences or thin populations
