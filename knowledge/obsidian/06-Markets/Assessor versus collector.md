# Assessor versus collector

Assessment records and tax-collection records answer different questions. A assessed value is not a payoff balance or evidence that taxes are current.

## 101XVC workflow

- Identify each custodial office
- Preserve assessment year and tax period
- Reconcile parcel identity across systems
- Request a dated payoff or collection status when needed
