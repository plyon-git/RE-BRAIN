# Margins of error

Survey margins of error communicate uncertainty in estimates. They belong beside the estimate in downstream tables.

## 101XVC workflow

- Preserve estimate and uncertainty fields
- Flag suppressed and annotated values
- Check whether comparisons are statistically meaningful
- Avoid treating a county survey number as a precise parcel fact
