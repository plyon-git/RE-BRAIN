# Repeat sales methodology

Repeat-sales indexes use repeat observations to estimate price changes while applying a specified sample and statistical method.

## 101XVC workflow

- Read coverage and exclusions
- Check how geography and property mix affect representativeness
- Do not apply national change blindly to a local niche
- Document any time adjustment derived from the series
