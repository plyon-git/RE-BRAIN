# Market boundary

A useful market boundary follows substitutable demand and property characteristics rather than an arbitrary postal radius.

## 101XVC workflow

- State the target tenant or purchaser
- Compare competing submarkets and legal use
- Save geography IDs and boundary versions
- Review whether the boundary changes comp selection
