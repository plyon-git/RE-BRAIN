# Delinquent tax records

A delinquency record is dated evidence about a tax account. Status can change through payment, appeal, transfer, installment plan, or correction.

## 101XVC workflow

- Store tax year and record-as-of date
- Preserve raw account and parcel fields
- Verify current status before acting
- Keep charges, penalties, and total balance separate
