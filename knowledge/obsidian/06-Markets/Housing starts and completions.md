# Housing starts and completions

Construction surveys distinguish beginning a project from completing units, and are generally not an exact parcel inventory.

## 101XVC workflow

- Check coverage and regional granularity
- Separate single-family and multifamily units
- Use revisions consistently
- Compare completions with local absorption evidence
