# Counterparty risk

Counterparty risk is the chance that another party cannot or will not perform its contractual obligations.

## 101XVC workflow

- Identify actual contracting entities
- Verify funding and operating capacity
- Map guarantees and termination rights
- Track concentration by counterparty and end purchaser
