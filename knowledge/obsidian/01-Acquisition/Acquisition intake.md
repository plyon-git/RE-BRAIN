# Acquisition intake

An intake record should make the asset, property interest, seller authority, requested economics, and next decision unambiguous.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record county and parcel ID before comparing similarly named addresses
- Separate seller statements from verified records
- Save contract deadlines and the responsible reviewer
- Retain the source and date for each important field
