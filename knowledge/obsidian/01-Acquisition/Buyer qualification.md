# Buyer qualification

A disposition buyer is qualified by credible funding, fit with the asset, execution capacity, and the contract terms they accept.

## 101XVC workflow

- Match legal buyer name to proof of funds
- Verify financing contingencies and available cash
- Record prior completed purchases rather than verbal volume claims
- Distinguish a prospective buyer from a closed counterparty
