# Contract assignment

Assignment transfers a contractual interest subject to the executed agreement and applicable jurisdictional requirements.

## 101XVC workflow

- Read assignment restrictions and consent requirements
- Identify the interest marketed and use written disclosures where required
- Track assignee performance and assignor obligations
- Ask closing counsel to confirm the transfer and funding sequence
