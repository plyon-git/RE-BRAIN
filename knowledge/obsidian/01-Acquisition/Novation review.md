# Novation review

A proposed novation requires explicit legal review of which obligations and parties are replaced. A label cannot substitute for executed terms.

## 101XVC workflow

- Describe the intended seller, buyer, listing, and payment relationships
- Document consent and release provisions
- Review brokerage licensing and marketing authority
- Model compensation only from the signed transaction documents
