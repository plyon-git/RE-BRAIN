# Earnest money

Earnest money is a transaction deposit whose holder, release conditions, and treatment at closing come from the contract.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record deposit due date and escrow recipient
- Track refundable and nonrefundable periods
- Treat credited deposits consistently in cash-to-close calculations
- Record forfeitures as realized costs rather than successful transactions
