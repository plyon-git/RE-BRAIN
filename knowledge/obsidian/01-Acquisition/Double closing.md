# Double closing

A double closing involves two conveyances. Its viability depends on funding, sequencing, disclosure, title, and the actual contracts.

## 101XVC workflow

- Model both settlements and all closing charges
- Confirm permitted use and timing of funds with settlement counsel
- Do not assume the second buyer finances the first purchase
- Reconcile both final statements after closing
