# Proof of funds

Proof of funds is dated evidence of accessible capital. A document generated by the seller is not independent evidence of the buyer's liquidity.

## 101XVC workflow

- Record issuer, account owner, currency, and verification route
- Redact unnecessary account identifiers in shared copies
- Check restrictions and document age
- Confirm funds availability through an authorized channel
