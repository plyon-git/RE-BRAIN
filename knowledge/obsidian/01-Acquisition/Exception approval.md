# Exception approval

An exception record makes the accepted risk, approving authority, and expiry visible when a deal falls outside ordinary underwriting.

## 101XVC workflow

- Preserve the original failed rule
- Write the compensating evidence and downside
- Require named approval and a timestamp
- Review recurring exceptions for a policy defect
