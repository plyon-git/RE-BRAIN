# Conversion funnel

A conversion funnel uses dated, observable stages to distinguish leads, qualified opportunities, signed contracts, funded closings, and collected revenue.

## 101XVC workflow

- Define denominators and exit evidence for each stage
- Measure mature cohorts with consistent observation windows
- Retain cancellations and failed closings
- Report both unit conversion and net economics after acquisition spending
