# Buy box

A buy box is an explicit screen for acceptable geography, asset type, price, condition, liquidity, and capital requirements.

## 101XVC workflow

- Document exclusions and exception authority
- Keep screening thresholds versioned
- Backtest both accepted and rejected leads
- Require deeper review when evidence is missing
