# Disposition pipeline

A disposition pipeline should separate outreach, indications, contracts, scheduled settlements, and completed closings.

## 101XVC workflow

- Use stages with observable exit criteria
- Record failed closings and reasons
- Compute conversion from cohorts with equal observation windows
- Recognize deal revenue only using the accounting policy and settlement evidence
