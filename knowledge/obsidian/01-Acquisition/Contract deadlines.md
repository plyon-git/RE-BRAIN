# Contract deadlines

The decision clock is defined by the signed agreement and amendments, not the date a lead enters the CRM.

## 101XVC workflow

- Track inspection, financing, title, deposit, and closing dates separately
- Store timezone and business-day convention
- Set escalation before expiration
- Require executed evidence for deadline extensions
