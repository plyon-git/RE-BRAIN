# Parcel identity

A street address is a label; the legal parcel and conveyed property rights determine what is being bought.

## 101XVC workflow

- Keep county parcel IDs as jurisdiction-qualified text
- Compare legal description with survey and contract
- Identify multiple parcels, condominium units, and excluded acreage
- Preserve historic parcel splits and merges
