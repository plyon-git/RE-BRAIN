# Seller authority

The party willing to sell must have authority to transfer the offered interest. A mailing name alone does not establish that authority.

## 101XVC workflow

- Match vesting and execution names
- Request entity or fiduciary authority documents
- Flag estate, trust, bankruptcy, and co-owner issues
- Have the settlement professional resolve inconsistencies before committing nonrefundable funds
